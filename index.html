How the night goes

Single elimination: win and you move on, lose and you’re out. Every result goes up live on the big screen.

Register

Scan the QR code on the poster and lock in your spot. Only 32 players per division.

Pay R100

Settle your entry fee at the venue before the draw.

The draw

Opponents are drawn at random and your bracket appears on the TV.

Play your racks

Each match is a set number of racks, announced on the night. Winners advance automatically.

Take the crown

The top 3 take home cash and the champion is named King or Queen of the Family.
